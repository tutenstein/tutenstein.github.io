Sorting area

Right parcel, right shelf, happy customer

Drag each parcel by its label to the right bin — standard delivery, branch pickup, fragile or priority. Busy? Let your clerk sort a batch for you.

  • Hands-on sorting that is always safe
  • Staff batches are faster to start, but a stressed clerk may drop a fragile parcel
  • Keep the shelves from overflowing before the next truck
Counter

Every parcel has a story

Customers queue up to send and collect parcels. Weigh the box, put the label on the right side, check the recipient's ID — and keep the line moving.

  • Quick counter mini-games: scale, label and ID check
  • A cast of regulars, each waiting their turn
  • Upgrades like the auto scale speed things up
Out in the neighborhood

Ride along with your couriers

Pick a vehicle and a route, then follow the delivery live through the streets of a little harbor town.

Delivery

Ringing the bell, stop by stop

Parcels belong to neighborhoods. Load your courier, choose how they ride, and watch them make every stop. Rain slows routes down; a good plan gets everyone home on time.

  • Couriers on foot, e-bike and more as you grow
  • Five live street scenes
  • The “you weren't home” shortcut is always there — but customers remember
