Everything for the theory test in one app

Made for everyone learning to drive in Finland – including newcomers learning Finnish traffic rules in a new language. Switch the language at any time, and view the original Finnish text of any translated question.

Mock exam built to the official structure

Practise under real conditions before the actual test.

  • 70 tasks in three parts, under one 30-minute clock
  • 15 category B questions, 50 traffic situations and 5 hazard perception tasks
  • Each part is passed or failed on its own
  • Review every answer afterwards

989 practice questions with explanations

Practice questions in the style of the theory test, written in Finnish and translated into English and Swedish.

  • 289 rule questions, 126 hazard perception tasks and 574 picture tasks
  • 1,247 traffic images – open them full screen and zoom in
  • 14 theory topics with study summaries and 112 explained questions
  • Weak spots: practise again the questions you got wrong

All 373 Finnish road signs

Every official sign and variant under the Road Traffic Act 729/2018, in the nine families A–I.

  • Official sign code and name in English, Finnish and Swedish
  • Explanations for the 164 signs in families A–E
  • Search by name or code, and learn with flashcards

Play & Learn in a little city

Drive a little blue car through an illustrated city seen from above and decide what to do next. Every decision comes with an explanation and the rule behind it – and there is no countdown.

  • 140 traffic situations across 8 neighbourhoods
  • Traffic lights, priority, pedestrians, cyclists, lanes, parking and more

Track your progress
