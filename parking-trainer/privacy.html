Privacy Policy

Parking Trainer · Last updated: 13 October 2026

Parking Trainer does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app does

Parking Trainer is a parking simulator with lessons. Everything runs on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Data stored on your device

Your lesson results and settings (language, controls, graphics) are saved locally on your device so they are there next time. They never leave the device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Network use

The app works offline. The web version loads its own files and fonts from the page you opened it on; nothing is sent back.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions: see the support page.
