Learn, then test

Every lesson has a coach with guide lines and a demo that drives it for you — watch the car, the wheel and the mirrors. Then take the test with no help and earn up to three stars.

  • Step-by-step coaching: when to stop, where to turn, when to straighten
  • A demo for every lesson
  • Scored on lines, angle, direction and mirror checks
  • Practise with the space on your left or your right

A real cockpit

The controls work like a car's, so the habits you build here carry over to the road.

  • A steering wheel you turn with your finger — 2¾ turns lock to lock, and it stays where you leave it
  • Analog gas and brake pedals, an automatic P R N D selector and a handbrake
  • Live door mirrors that tilt down in reverse
  • Tap a mirror to check it — reversing without a mirror check costs points, just like a real test

See it from every angle

Switch cameras at any time. From the driver's seat you can even look over your shoulder.

Honest physics

The car is a low-speed model that behaves like a real car at parking speed, so what works here works on the road.
